The SVG is the editable source; [01-indexed-d-thin-filled-rows.png](01-indexed-d-thin-filled-rows.png) is a 640 × 640 white-background preview.

The enclosing D retains the exact V13 path, 9-unit stroke and blue `#0874F9`. The row colors remain green `#21A668`, blue `#0874F9` and red `#D83B42`.

The three pill bodies are 10 units high, reduced from 14, with circular 5-unit end caps. Their centers remain y = 62, 90 and 118, and their horizontal extents remain x = 29–124, 29–134 and 29–120. Each tab rises 6 units above its body, reduced from 9. The top-left corners use circular 5-unit arcs; each row remains one closed, seamless contour with a softened tab shoulder.

The SVG has a transparent background. Earlier logo versions and application code are unchanged.
